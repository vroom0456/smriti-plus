## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a dignifying, warm, and restorative digital sanctuary for older adults experiencing cognitive changes, alongside their families and caregivers. Instead of relying on sterile clinical diagnostic tropes or infantilizing gamification, the interface blends cognitive clarity with subtle Northeast Indian regional craftsmanship—evoking the misted serenity of Assam tea hills, structural bamboo lattice geometry, and the respectful grace of the traditional *xorai* and *jaapi*.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Serene & Natural Dignity:** Surfaces mimic tactile, warm handmade paper and sunlit ivory textiles, avoiding harsh luminescent clinical whites that trigger eye strain and cognitive fatigue.
- **Architectural Harmony & Order:** Layouts adopt balanced, symmetrical structures reminiscent of traditional woven weaves (*eri* & *muga* silk borders), bringing predictable visual anchors and calm orientation.
- **Supportive Tactility:** Touch surfaces, target zones, and status cards employ soft organic curvature, clear physical cues, and comforting depth to assure trembling or hesitant interactions.
- **Affirming Intelligence:** Celebratory moments, reminders, and memory games are underscored with amber warmth and verdant tea-leaf hues, communicating vitality, growth, and gentle reassurance.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for high visual legibility, circadian warmth, and emotional reassurance, complying strictly with WCAG AAA standards (7:1 contrast ratio) for core informational text and WCAG AA (4.5:1) for interactive elements.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Surface Base:** Deep ivory and warm unbleached silk (`#fbfaf7` for base canvas; `#ffffff` for elevated primary cards; `#f4f0ea` for inset trays and secondary surfaces). Completely avoids stark `#000000` on `#ffffff`.
- **Primary Anchor (Brahmaputra Midnight):** Deep indigo navy (`#172554`). Imparts medical authority, stability, and enduring clarity across headings, primary action buttons, and dominant landmarks.
- **Secondary Accent (Tea Garden Teal):** Lush herbal tea green and calming deep teal (`#0f766e`, with lighter interactive tier `#14b8a6`). Represents neural health, vitality, daily task completion, and refreshing calm.
- **Tertiary Accent (Muga Amber & Terracotta):** Warm mustard and terracotta gold (`#b45309`, with soft tint `#fef3c7`). Used purposefully for medication alerts, memory streak achievements, daily reminders, and gentle attention cues without inducing panic.
- **Neutral Hierarchy:** Warm stone neutrals (`#292524` for high-clarity body copy, `#57534e` for supportive labels, `#a8a29e` for passive borders, and `#e7e2d9` for delicate geometric separators).

## Typography

The type system prioritizes legibility, high x-height, and generous letter tracking to prevent visual fatigue and support presbyopia. **Plus Jakarta Sans** is employed systematically for its humanist geometric rhythm, open counters, and warm, friendly terminals.

### Typographic Principles
- **Generous Line Heights:** Text blocks use a minimum of 1.5× line height (`lineHeight: 26px` on `16px` body) to prevent reading line jumps common with age-related visual degradation.
- **Minimum Interactive Sizes:** Interactive labels never dip below `14px`, ensuring clarity for buttons, navigational targets, and input placeholders.
- **Spacious Tracking:** Micro-copy and captions utilize subtle positive letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to prevent adjacent character blending.
- **Weight Restraint:** Light weights below `400` are forbidden to prevent low-contrast rendering on varied displays. High-contrast bolding is preserved for primary anchors and section demarcations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy utilizes a structured, human-scale fixed-fluid hybrid grid anchored by generous negative space. Cluttered layouts provoke anxiety; this system intentionally embraces expansive margins and dedicated "breathing pockets."

### Grid and Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (20px) margins and `1rem` gutters. All primary action targets span the full grid width or operate as persistent bottom-docked action bars.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Splits cognitive exercises and caregiver overviews into 2 stable side-by-side modules.
- **Desktop / Competition Display (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` max-width with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters, keeping interfaces centralized and eliminating excessive horizontal eye travel.

### Interaction Guardrails
- **The Golden Target Rule:** Minimum interactive touch target is set to `52px` (`3.25rem`), significantly exceeding the standard 48px baseline to accommodate mild tremors or motor stiffness.
- **Separation Rhythm:** Interactive controls maintain a mandatory minimum clearance of `12px` (`0.75rem`) between each other to eliminate accidental activations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, sunlit ambient shadowing paired with tactile surface layering, avoiding high-contrast floating gimmicks. Elevation mirrors natural light falling across physical paper, hand-pressed parchment, and sanded bamboo trays.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Warm Ivory (`#fbfaf7`). The foundational ground for the entire experience.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Pure Linen White (`#ffffff`) resting over ivory, structured with a hairline border (`1px solid #ede8df`) and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -4px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements):** Active modules, modal dialogs, and persistent bottom sheets utilize an expanded warm diffusion: `0 12px 36px -6px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.03)` with a subtle inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Urgent Callouts & Alerts):** Grounded using soft tertiary halos (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.12)`) to draw gaze gently without shock or alarm.

## Shapes

The shape system employs soft, organic geometry that feels approachable, safe, and modern. Sharp corners (`0px`) are entirely avoided to prevent subconscious apprehension or clinical severity.

### Radius Architecture
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Subtle tags, internal badge icons, and input indicators.
- **Form Controls & Buttons (`1rem` / 16px):** Primary interactive touchpoints, pills, and selectable tiles.
- **Primary Cards & Modals (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Memory recall cards, wellness exercise modules, and daily routine panels.
- **Floating Action Nodes (`9999px`):** Audio assistant prompts, quick emergency caregiver links, and primary bottom navigators.
- **Cultural Texture:** Subtly integrated geometric border accents evoke traditional Assam woven diamond borders (*phool jali*) on card footers and divider lines using faint 12% opacity neutral strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled deep navy (`#172554`) with pure white text (`#ffffff`), `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`), `56px` minimum height, and `24px` horizontal padding. On press, transitions to `#1e295d` with an inner shadow.
- **Secondary Accent Button:** Tea Garden Teal (`#0f766e`) filled or soft teal tint (`#f0fdfa` background with `#0f766e` text and `1.5px` border in `#ccfbf1`). Used for positive reinforcement actions (e.g., "Start Memory Game").
- **Tertiary & Utility Button:** Warm ivory tint (`#f4f0ea`) with `#292524` text, no hard shadow, gentle hover shift. For non-urgent secondary choices (e.g., "Review Later").
- **Accessibility Feature:** All buttons feature an explicit `3px` focus ring in `#0f766e` with a `2px` white offset for keyboard and screen-switch navigation.

### Cards & Daily Prompts
- **Memory & Exercise Cards:** Linen white backdrop with `24px` radius, bordered by a soft `1px` stroke of `#ede8df`. Top-accented with an understated 4px woven color rail indicating activity category (e.g., Teal for Logic, Amber for Reminiscence).
- **Caregiver Alert Card:** Light warm amber tint (`#fffbeb`) with a soft border (`#fde68a`), containing high-contrast text (`#92400e`), an reassuring icon, and a prominent one-tap call button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Generous height (`54px`), `16px` corner radius, warm ivory background (`#fcfbf9`), and a crisp `1.5px` resting border in `#d6d0c4`. On focus, transitions to a solid `2px` border in Midnight Navy (`#172554`) with a soft teal aura.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Scaled up to a tactile `28px × 28px` container. Selected state fills with Midnight Navy (`#172554`) or Herbal Teal (`#0f766e`) featuring a bold, crisp 3px white checkmark to ensure unambiguous visual status.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height of `36px`, rounded pill (`9999px`), light natural background with contrasting semi-bold label (`14px`). Accompanied by a distinct `8px` solid circular status dot (Teal for "Completed", Amber for "Upcoming", Slate for "Rest").

### Cognitive Specialty Modules
- **Voice Companion Indicator:** Pulsing circular disc (`64px`) surrounded by two concentric, translucent rings of Herbal Teal, mimicking peaceful meditative breathing rhythms (4-second inhale, 4-second exhale cycle).
- **Reminiscence Photo Anchor:** Full-bleed rounded card with high-contrast text overlay supported by an organic gradient scrim (`from-black/70 to-transparent`) ensuring readability over family photographs.
